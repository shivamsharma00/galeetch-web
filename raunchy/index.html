Free · macOS

Raunchy lives on your Mac.

A 3D raccoon who climbs your windows, naps draped over their edges, and steals your buttons. Catch him and he gives them back. Mostly.

Download for Mac All releases

macOS 13 Ventura or later · Apple silicon · signed & notarized by Apple

What he gets up to

Climbs everything

Hops up, hooks a hand over a line of text or a toolbar, hangs, and hauls himself over the top.

Steals your buttons

Grabs a button or a word, leaves a bite hole and tows it off. Click him to get it back. The real button is never touched.

Naps on ledges

Lies down on a window top and lets his legs slide off one by one, like a raccoon on a branch. Dreams, kicks, sighs.

Likes being petted

Move the cursor over him and he leans in, eyes shut, chittering. Ignore him for a day and he sulks.

Water fight

Shake the mouse and the cursor becomes a water pistol. He runs, dives behind windows, digs into a ledge, and learns your tricks.

Has manners

Steps away when you share your screen or go full-screen, sits quietly on calls. ⌃⌥⌘P sends him away and back.

Install

  1. Download Raunchy.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag Raunchy into Applications, then open him from there.
  3. He shows up on your screen and lives in the menu bar as 🦝. Settings, size, sound and “Open at login” are all in that menu.
  4. Optional: switch on mischief in Settings to let him walk on toolbars and steal buttons. macOS will ask for Accessibility access then, not before.

Privacy

Raunchy runs entirely on your Mac. Nothing is sent anywhere, there are no accounts and no analytics.

Accessibility (optional) lets him see where buttons, toolbars and text are in the front window so he can climb and steal them. He never changes anything in other apps, and he stops looking whenever a password field is focused.

To remove him, quit from the 🦝 menu and drag Raunchy from Applications to the Trash.
